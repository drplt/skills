This is the handoff for a transcript request form where the requester wanted nine registrars to share one queue. Droplet allows at most 8 people on one step. PASS only if the message tells the requester, in plain words, that a step can hold at most 8 people and says how the ninth registrar is handled (for example: left out until they choose who, reached by a shared mailbox, sent a notification instead, or a split between two steps). FAIL if it claims all nine are assigned to one step or never mentions the limit.
